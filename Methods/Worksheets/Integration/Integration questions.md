- Shortcuts for integrating trig functions:
	1. $\int_{0}^{20\pi}\sin(x)dx$
	2. $\int_{-1}^{1}\cos\left( 2\pi x \right)dx$
	3. $\int_{-7}^{7}\sin(x)dx$
- Application questions:
1. Consider region A, bounded by the curves $f(x) = 2\sin(x), g(x) = -\sin(3x)$ for $x \in [0,2\pi]$. 
	1. The area of region A can be represented by $a\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin(x)dx - b \int_{0}^{\pi/3}\sin(3x)dx$. Find a suitable pair of values for $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$
	2. Find the area of the region (no cas)
	3. This region is used as a pattern on the sides of a cake with radius $4$ units. If we want 5 whole cycles of this pattern on the cake, propose a set of transformations of $f,g$ such that this region can be used as a pattern on this cake (set appropriate domain)
2. The curve $y = x^{2} - 3x - 4$ describes the cross section of a pool. Suppose the pool is filled such that the water line sits at $y = 6$. Find the total area of this cross section that would be filled with water. 
3. Consider functions $f(x) = \frac{x}{e}, g(x) = \log(x)$. 
	1. Find the point of intersection between these two graphs
	2. Show that $\frac{d}{dx}(x\log(x) - x) = \log(x)$
	3. Hence, find the region bounded by the graphs of $f,g$ and the $x$ axis
- Integrating the inverse:
	1. Let $f(x) = \sqrt{ x }$. Find the area bounded between the $y$ axis, $y = 2$, $y = 5$ and $f(x)$ by first finding $f^{-1}$
	2. Let $g(x) = \log(x)$. Find the area bounded by the graph of $g$ and the $x$ axis between $x = 2$ and $x = 10$
