1. Let $f_k: [0,\infty)\to R , f_k(x)=x^ke^{-x}, k \in \mathbb{Z} ^+$. The graph of $f_{k}$ has points of inflections at points $P$ and $Q$, where $x = p$ and $x = q$ respectively. Note that $0<p<q$. 
	1. Find $p$ and $q$ in terms of $k$
	2. Find $x_{m}$, the $x$ coordinate of the midpoint of the line segment $PQ$.
	3. Hence show that the maximum stationary point of the graph has coordinates $(x_{m}, f_{k}(x_{m}))$
2. Evaluate $\int_{0}^{\pi/3} \tan^{2}(x) + \tan^{4}(x)dx$
3. Evaluate
	1. $\int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \cos^{-1}(2x)dx$
	2. $\int_{-1}^{2}\sin^{-1}\left( \frac{x}{2} \right)$
4. Consider the function $g(x) = p \tan(x) + q \sec(x)$ over its maximal domain, where $0<q<p$. Show that the range of $g$ is $\mathbb{R}$
5. Let $g(x) = \sqrt[n]{a^{n} - x^{n}  }$ defined over its maximal domain, $a \in \mathbb{R}$. For what values of  $x$ will $g(x) = g^{-1}(x)$? 
6. Consider the Cartesian relation $S: (x-\sqrt{ 2 })^{2} + (y - \sqrt{ 2 })^{2} = 2$
	1. Express $S$ in the complex plane in two different ways
	2. Find the points of intersection of $S$ and graph of the relation $z \bar{z} = 2$ in cartesian form.
	3. Find the common area enclosed by the graph of $S$ and the graph of the relation $z \bar{z} = 2$
7. Suppose $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $z^{2} - 4 \cos(\alpha)z + 4 = 0$ where $\alpha  \in \left( 0, \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$
	1. Find the roots $z_{1}, z_{2}$ of this equation in terms of $\alpha$ in polar form
	2. Find the value of $\alpha$ for which $|Arg\left( \frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}} \right)| = \frac{5\pi}{6}$
8. Find the Area of the major segment bounded by the line passing through the roots of $z^{2} + 4z + 16 = 0$ and the major arc of the circle given by $|z |= 4$
