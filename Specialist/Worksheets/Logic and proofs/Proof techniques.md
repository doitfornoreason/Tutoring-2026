- Mixed proof questions, not grouped by techniques so that you can practice finding the correct technique yourself
---
1. For $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$, prove that if $x + y$ is odd, then $x$ and $y$ are opposite in parity (parity = odd or even)
2. Prove or disprove: Product of two irrational numbers is not always irrational
3. Prove that $n^{3} - 4n$ is divisible by $3$ for every integer $n$
4. Let $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$ prove the following: $ab = 4 \implies (a-b)^{3} - 9(a - b) = 0$  
5. Prove that if $a$ is an odd integer, then $5a^{2} - 3a + 5$ is odd
6. Prove or disprove: there is no largest negative rational number
7. Prove or disprove: the sum of two irrational numbers is always irrational
8. For $a,b,c \in \mathbb{Z}$ Prove that $a ^2 + b^{2} = c^{2} \implies abc$ is even
9. Rest of the examples on the SUP :)
